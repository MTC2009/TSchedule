TSchedule is an independent, iPad-only student companion. The native app supports English and Simplified Chinese using the iPadOS app language setting. School-supplied content and the official Xiaobao website are not translated. It does not create a separate TSchedule account, sell access, or submit or edit school records.

Live academic records require an existing student account on the supported school portal. Users connect through the school’s official web page inside the app. The authenticated session is used to read the current student’s authorized timetable, tasks, released grades, and attendance. The native app does not send those records or portal credentials to a developer-operated server.

My Tasks shows when the last complete semester list was checked. The protected on-device copy is excluded from backup and does not include grades, feedback or attachments. Optional assignment reminders are scheduled locally and use a generic title unless the student chooses otherwise. Changes and withdrawals reach the device on the next successful sync. Switching students or signing out revokes the old copy and its reminders.

There is a separate optional saved sign-in in Settings > Personal Info > Campus Data (设置 > 个人信息 > 校园资料). It is off by default. If a student explicitly enables it, the account credential is kept in the device-only Keychain and may be used once for eligible reauthentication on the official portal: on a foreground launch if expressly enabled, or on a deliberate grade visit. Recovery itself verifies identity only. A separately verified current session may refresh the published overview while a cached Today is visible, without claiming a password. The device timetable persists across launches, is excluded from backup and is revoked at in-app sign-out or account change. It can be removed in Settings. The basic portal login does not require this feature.

Weather optionally requests a fixed Dongguan city report from Open-Meteo; it does not access the device’s location or send academic records to the weather provider.
