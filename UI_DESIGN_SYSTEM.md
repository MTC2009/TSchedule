# iPadOS UI design system

Scope: the native SwiftUI app. Existing navigation and student pages are the baseline; the Rust service does not own presentation.

- **App identity:** The sidebar navigation title pairs the approved campus mark (24 pt, continuous 5 pt corners) with the TSchedule wordmark in the native headline style. Treat them as one noninteractive accessible heading, not an extra toolbar button. The light and dark images share the same campus silhouette and adapt to the effective interface appearance. With Follow System selected, the in-app mark follows the system color scheme; do not override the app’s other appearance settings. The system app icon uses the same unmasked variants and respects the OS icon appearance. Let standard notifications use the system-supplied icon and name; do not duplicate them in notification content. Keep the school login and portal actions labeled 校宝.
- **Lock Screen lesson activity:** On the left pair the campus mark and a caption with the large whole-minute countdown. Don't repeat the system clock, show a wall-clock end time, or display seconds. The next-course heading and large name are both right aligned. Use a single line with a tail ellipsis, keep the full title accessible, and let the card open Today rather than forcing a scrolling animation. Default to the system surface; the optional activity backgrounds and the Settings sample share the documented activity colors. Label demos, clear details on expiry, and offer one explicit start/stop action in Today.
- **Cached timetable:** Today and Planner paint the same saved year before live-session checks and keep that display during temporary system deactivation. Keep the sole **更新课表** action in the existing Settings account/data group. Native button, pending/disabled state and a plain-language last-update or failure label; do not introduce an offline login sheet or imply a missing Day is free.
- **School data source:** GPA entry uses the verified account and selected semester, with a protected last-success snapshot; explicit refresh requests published grades without replacing the populated view while pending or after a transient error; do not add a separate permission button. Distinguish a loading document, confirmed expiry, a failed read and a successful empty result. A foreground recovery does not present a web sheet; offer interactive sign-in only when needed. On GPA the action is 刷新 GPA, not an authorization. Show attendance as native data using the shared filters, summary, and course table. Use the school-provided overall rate or show it as unavailable; keep empty, filtered-empty and failed reads distinct. Only unintegrated destinations retain explicit website actions. The optional demo data service has its own initial grade-read flow.
- **Settings:** Keep one sidebar item and a grouped native Form with Appearance, Live Activity, Reminders, Personal Info and Privacy routes, plus the pre-existing grade tools and version. Use the shared activity card as a clearly named sample. Opt-in, secure input and removal stay together with account status and one website action under Settings → 个人信息 → 校园资料, separate from viewing GPA. Never show a password as ordinary text or solicit enrollment on every grade visit. Use a native opt-in switch and lead picker for confirmed device-cached classes. Explain a missing timetable or system permission inline. An independent plan reminder and its subtitle never configure class alerts; do not show class titles or rooms in these notifications. At narrow widths and large text retain native form wrapping, focus treatments and disclosure behavior.
- **Planner school times:** In both day and week, keep exact start and end times off school cards and Free Blocks. The common ruler and card geometry do not change. In Browse, tap to see the times in the existing course or exam detail, or in the shared native timetable sheet. Only point-in-time entries without an interval need the small popover; note and academic labels appear only in their applicable records. Short school entries display their name (grouped names if necessary), not a white circular glyph. All meals share the standard card; compact meal text must fit within its designated interval. The cards stay passive during Edit and range selection.
- **Grade provenance:** In the connected GPA overview, show the school’s published GPA by default. When the student saves a course forecast, label the hypothetical course totals and distinct forecast GPA; keep Restore Published Grades beside Refresh. If none is published, label a credit-weighted estimate or name the missing inputs next to the readout. Keep trial controls in Settings. A confirmed pending-publication task shows 「已批改，待发布」. Show a separately released 「预估分」 only in task detail, with 「非正式成绩」 next to it; cards and GPA do not use it.
- **Student-facing copy:** Name the course, time, action or result rather than the build or fixture behind it. Today opens at the school clock; a selected time is identified only while exploring. Explain synthetic academic data in Settings → 个人信息 → 校园资料. Keep consequential qualifiers at the point of use (local-only completion, estimated GPA, cached weather, unavailable records). Never strip words from school-authored material.
- **Schedule navigation:** The Campus section has Today for the current school day and Planner for browsing dates and personal plans; there is no standalone timetable page. All top-level toolbars use the same text shortcut: today's confirmed `Day N`, or today's Shanghai date when no Day is known. It opens Planner at today, even if Planner was showing another date. After the last visible period or on a day with no remaining visible periods, the existing focus card names the next school date and its known rotation, first lesson and room. Its single action opens that exact date; when no date is confirmed show text, not a dead button. VoiceOver reads the summary separately from the action.
- **Today hierarchy:** The first content heading is the resolved rotation day in primary text with the school date/weekday secondary. A confirmed non-teaching day has a rest-day heading if no Day was printed; an unloaded timetable has no invented day. Keep the number off the timeline. Let the date move to a second line at narrow widths or large text sizes, and expose the pair as one VoiceOver heading.
- **Today layout:** Use one course card above one draggable timeline, with no parallel upcoming list. Keep the card and timeline in place when switching between a lesson, a break and the end of the day; measure the content at the available width and text size rather than imposing a fixed pixel height. Put the selected time on the timeline, not in a second caption. The sky still reflects the weather; its condition, source and update status belong in the environment panel rather than the Today footer.
- **Today timetable card:** Courses, campus activities, a Free Block and a confirmed Break all share a status with countdown, blue location when known, actual item name, and a separate exact time range. In English, assembly uses “Homeroom/Theater” and “Homeroom / School Assembly”; lunch uses “Canteen” and “Lunch”. A Free Block or Break has no blue location. Lunch and Break count down to the next actual class or assembly, not to a vacant slot; if no instruction follows, name the transition instead. Non-course cards have no chevron, detail action, room placeholder or lesson reminder.
- **Timeline ink:** Each tile centers a single name; rooms stay in the focus card and the timetable, not on the strip at any width. Courses, assembly, lunch and confirmed Free Blocks all use the same clock: future tiles are neutral, current tiles fill left to right and ended tiles are fully blue. This is elapsed time, not student completion or attendance. Non-course tiles have no detail action. The time lens is the single adjustable control; it speaks the active title and elapsed percentage.
- **Timeline spacing:** One strip, no second ruler. Allocate tile width in proportion to actual duration and use a uniform 6 pt visual separator (smaller only when necessary to keep at least 75% of a very narrow strip for its contents). A confirmed Break is presented above the strip when its minute is selected in the separator; it does not add a tile. A separator between touching blocks represents only their shared instant. If source lessons actually overlap, preserve their real time coordinates rather than invent a new order.
- **Timeline lens:** At rest, keep a 2 pt position line in an 8 × 54 pt glass strip; the footer gives the exact time. Contact anywhere on the track expands it to 62 × 84 pt and reveals the time in the lens, even without a drag. Hover alone doesn't expand it. Release or interruption collapses the glass, not the selected time. Keep the 94 pt track fixed and fully interactive in both states. Respect Reduce Motion and use a solid system surface for Reduce Transparency.
- **Planner:** Use the same school glass material and rounded date heading as the existing pages. School cards, including Free Blocks in the very same material and primary semibold type, occupy the full track and are positioned using only school entries. Keep due-only school records in their dedicated pages; only scheduled school entries and school notices belong on this board alongside personal plans. Paint each day's personal plan ranges **above** the school cards and their text on the same clock, across the full track; never reserve a separate column or draw a duplicate wash. Saved overlaps gain tint only inside the overlap, under the [color system](COLOR_SYSTEM.md) cap. The paint passes taps to the course underneath. Place bare icon–title labels around the measured school text; ellipsize titles before falling back to individual icons. When neighboring annotations cannot remain legible, show their numbered icons in one Liquid Glass capsule. Its popover lists every plan, and each row opens its own editor. Keep time bands and the 4 pt school card spacing independent of the labels.
- **Planner editing:** Browse by default; swipes scroll and course actions still work. Tap an existing timeline plan label or group row to open its form without entering board edit mode. A Browse-only eye in the Redo position toggles personal annotations and ranges without changing saved records or school notices. Entering Edit temporarily shows plans; leaving restores the prior Browse visibility. Undo and Redo are Edit-only; disable history when empty or during a write. The header and time ruler keep their geometry on a mode change. Both modes use the same secondary whole-hour labels and half-hour guides; Edit adds accessible tap targets without extra text. In Edit, hold a non-handle part of the track then drag to select a new range, or hold the handle and drag to move. In a very narrow annotation, one target supports tap to edit and hold to move. A held gesture owns one role, and ordinary swipes scroll. A new range fills the existing form; the ruler retains a two-tap alternative. Opening a form or a plan group does not edit anything.
- **Planner bubbles and ranges:** The pinned shelf, including its single add button, horizontally scrollable bubbles and any reminder status, appears only in Edit. Ordinary Browse reserves no shelf space. Choosing a range from the form automatically enters Edit, keeps the draft, and replaces the shelf with range instructions and Cancel. In the week view name the draft's date in that instruction; other columns cannot edit that draft's time. Return the range to the form without writing until Save. In the existing form, a native Repeat picker chooses no repetition, weekday, or rotation Day. Show only its matching value picker. A reusable non-recurring bubble creates an independent record. A recurring bubble or occurrence moves the series, using the existing repeat glyph and a spoken recurrence label; earlier elapsed dates stay intact. Moves use the target date, snap to five minutes and preserve duration. Only a previously untimed drop may inherit a confirmed Free Block, not a drawn range. Use translucent ranges with capped endpoint rules, a plan title/symbol and readable state text. Color alone never means completion, conflict or selection.
- **Planner week and month:** Wide landscape (usable pane at least 745 pt and wider than tall) shows the existing Sunday-first week; all seven equal-width drop targets align to one time ruler. Portrait and constrained or accessibility layouts keep the day. The month grid is Monday-first like the school publication. Distinguish no-class markings from event markings with both shape and accessible text; show a named holiday's full date span rather than treating every weekend or blank date as a closure. Day and week detail uses the same published HS notices without creating appointments.
- **Planner corners:** Use continuous rounded rectangles: 12 pt for timeline cards, status cards, the drag preview and band fill; 16 pt for the outer shelf or range selection controls; 8 pt for unscheduled cards outside the shelf. Use a capsule for the small draggable shelf bubbles. Keep native buttons and the other pages' corners unchanged.
- **GPA overview:** Use the Today card material for the overview and individual rounded course tiles, including its Reduce Transparency fallback. Stack the term-context heading above “GPA” on the left and keep the readout right-aligned. Term and source stay next to the readout. The eye is a small neutral symbol without a filled circle and has a 44 pt target. Hiding the readout removes the numeric string from accessibility without hiding course totals. The grid reduces its columns with available width and uses one column at accessibility sizes. Course tiles navigate; only grade availability and refresh controls follow the course totals. Both standalone semester trial forms live only in Settings → 成绩工具, as native Form rows without a separate card. Keep the GPA's source label and the separate course-level forecasts.
- **GPA course detail:** Group the small grade tile and the wider, tappable history preview into one row. Put the total's caption directly below its value, before any optional grade or point labels. Use the shared Today glass material for the three outer panels (grade tile, history preview and single task table), with 16 pt continuous corners and the existing opaque fallback for Reduce Transparency. Keep them separate in one glass container; table rows and subgroup insets do not add glass layers. On narrow windows or accessibility sizes stack the header and reflow rows if needed. Keep the preview free of axes and center it on the observations; show the full 0–100% scale, point selection and native school-date range controls in the enlarged chart. Only source groups with children disclose; inset children and name the weight scope when available. Use a 44 pt plus without a filled circle for the native forecast form; distinguish hypothetical rows and their result from published ones. Missing weights and scores never read as zero. The forecast form uses a native group menu and read-only share rows; nested groups distinguish their parent share from their effective course share.
- **My Tasks:** Use the GPA tile material and spacing for a compact grid, with the native course/type/mode/term/date/submission/grading controls (the deadline occupies the middle slot of the three-column second row; term uses the same labeled control as the other filters), title search and reset above it. Wrap the filters and narrow the grid instead of hiding options. Do not put a summary counter slab ahead of the cards. After the title and course/type, show a published score before the secondary deadline and teacher; omit the score row when there is no score. Clip the status ribbon to the bottom-trailing 30 pt tile corner. Set it about 40° from the horizontal and about 21 pt thick, with a centered symbol for each of the four colors. Keep the ribbon and duplicate status text out of the task score's space. The card still reads submission and grading separately through VoiceOver, and detail and filters keep their text labels. Local completion is device-only and does not change ribbon color. Only source-graded rows with a score show a number or letter. Show the latest publication first. Filtering or crossing a deadline must not change the order of surviving cards; undated portal rows stay last in the school's original list order. Use the same glass material in detail, with readable instruction, answer and evaluation sections; stack them when width or Dynamic Type requires it. Attachment names do not imply a download action.

- **Attendance:** Use two rows in the existing glass filter frame (12 pt inset, 18 pt continuous corners). Use two unstyled system segmented controls: give the category group 216 pt of width at default text size, with three equal segments, and place the 265 pt period group 10 pt after it. The period control can size segments to their titles. Keep selection effects, corner shapes and accessibility appearance native, with no extra glass behind either control or custom tint on selection. A custom date range leaves the period control in its native no-selection state. Keep the native compact date pickers on the second row. Let the groups reflow when the pane narrows or text size grows; scroll a group that does not fit on its own rather than compressing labels. At default text size, give the native controls 44 pt of height.
- **Attendance cards:** Keep the navigation title, but no duplicate header, count badges or explanatory footer; explain missing records where the user opens them. When the two cards sit side by side, the summary is 408 pt wide and both cards use the taller card's natural height. When the cards stack, both use the same available width. Place the ring on the left and the four status buttons in one column on the right whenever the stacked summary has room. At accessibility text sizes or in a very narrow pane, put the buttons below the ring instead. Preserve the ring's central rate, black callout, VoiceOver and keyboard selection, and the status buttons' course filtering. In both built-in and connected modes, show the course names, four labeled counts and present rate directly, without a second comparison mode or course search. The 16 pt muted bar in each row shows that course's composition without a bright rim or separate axis. Empty or invalid records have no bar. Preserve existing course navigation and reflow the table into labeled rows on narrow panes or at accessibility sizes.
- **Other Items (其余项目):** Keep one sidebar row with the native 考试 / 测评 / 资料库 segmented picker (a labeled menu at accessibility sizes). Use a compact leading filter panel, a short search next to the status dropdown or, for resources, next to the course dropdown. Wrap only as the pane narrows or when using accessibility type; do not add a total/status counter above the grid. Match My Tasks' 250 pt adaptive columns, 16 pt spacing and 30 pt glass corners. At regular type sizes, tile height is at least three quarters of the width and can grow with its contents. Do not reuse task submission ribbons. Start at 全部课程. A real course relation drives the filter; one multi-course exam still has one tile, and 未分科 only marks records without a course relation. Use readable text for status and local bookmarks. Back from a tile keeps the filters. Exam details emphasize time and room, if known; evaluations lead with status and deadline; resources give precedence to the original text with the existing local bookmark action. The selected section still owns the existing web action.
- **Other Items tile typography:** Use a semibold title, body-size course and time, and subheadline metadata. Use the tile's available height to separate the title and course from the timing and status below. Place the exam room and status on the same line when both fit; otherwise stack them. Let text wrap and tiles grow instead of shrinking the type.
- **Typography:** use SwiftUI semantic text styles and Dynamic Type. Keep numbers such as the background clock monospaced where they change. Use existing primary/secondary hierarchy instead of changing typography by weather.
- **Spacing, radius and borders:** keep the existing navigation, native Form rows, toolbar items and sheet detents. Reuse `GlassComponents.swift` for existing shared surfaces and accessibility fallback; don't restyle list content, shadows, outlines or corner radii for weather. Interactive targets should allow at least 44 pt. Existing environment time shortcuts use the native glass button style.
- **Responsive layout:** allow the iPad split view to compact using its system size class. The sky camera derives its aspect ratio from the viewport; the background must not influence content width. The environment weather picker uses native selection and grouped rows.
- **Interaction states:** use native Default, Selected, pressed and Focus treatments. Manual weather selection is synchronous; only an explicit choice leaves automatic mode. Automatic reads show the model's validity time, text for Pending/unavailable, or a timestamp labelled as cached. Keep this in the existing environment sheet, not a banner that competes with the student's work. Labels and selection indicators must not depend on color alone.
- **Backdrop motion:** the sidebar switches foreground pages over a single right-column sky; do not restart the cloud motion clock or fade the backdrop on each selection. Pause the covered main sky behind a detail and resume it on return. Generated volumetric clouds evolve independently of sky lighting. Never animate content because the sky changes. Pause the draw loop offscreen and honor Reduce Motion and Low Power Mode. Lightning uses a dim local branch, never a whole-screen flash.
- **Seasonal art direction (paused in the app; ordinary sky and weather remain visible):** the 13 holidays and 24 solar terms form one family of relief prints. Use a clear silhouette and connected ink values at a distance; reserve feather edges, leaf veins and carved details for subjects viewed close up. The natural sky keeps its own style. Use real Metal geometry for overlap, openings, reflection and moving light, not a screen-wide grade, billboard or photo. Airborne motifs share the scene with the moon rather than filling the screen. When using wildlife references, compare anatomy at a fixed camera; photographic imitation is not a requirement. Optical effects are not touch targets.
- **Accessibility:** sky pixels remain decorative, hidden from VoiceOver and never capture input. A passive, scoped recognizer may react to a visible seasonal subject or water touched in an unoccupied area. Foreground controls and vertical scrolling win; a tap also works without brushing. A stopped animation shows a static 3D pose rather than loading an image. Existing controls retain their semantic labels and contrast. Use [Color system](COLOR_SYSTEM.md) for art and readability washes.
