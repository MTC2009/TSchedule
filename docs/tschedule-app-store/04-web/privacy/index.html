TSchedule 是独立的 iPad 校园工具。本政策说明 App 及其支持邮件怎样处理信息。TSchedule 不提供独立用户注册，不是学校或校宝的官方应用。校园资料需要本人已有的学校账号。

校宝登录和校园资料

如选择连接学校，您在 App 内打开的校宝官方网页登录。校宝直接处理登录、会话和请求。App 在已登录会话中，只读取当前学生有权查看的课表、任务、已公布成绩、考勤等资料，用于在本机展示；不会改写学校记录，也不会把真实校宝凭据或学业资料转发给我们的服务器。原生页面的真实学业资料供当前会话使用，不建立独立长期成绩档案。校宝网页及 WebKit 会话可在设备上保留浏览器网站数据，具体取决于登录选项。

可选的本机代登录

这项功能默认关闭。如果您主动在「设置 → 个人信息 → 校园资料」开启，App 将核验过的校宝账号凭据存入此 iPad 的钥匙串，不跨设备同步。当您允许前台打开 App 时恢复登录，或主动进入或刷新 GPA，App 会先检查原会话；确实需要重新登录才向校宝官方页面使用该凭据一次。旧绑定不会自动扩大用途。凭据不用于后台轮询，也不会交给开发者。您可以点「移除已保存的登录」停止代登录，或退出校宝登录清理本机登录状态。

本机计划与天气

本机昵称、个人计划、外观和提醒保存在设备上。昵称不会改写校宝资料。手动开启课表实时活动时，下一节课程名、剩余分钟和自定义小标题可显示在锁屏上；是否显示通知内容取决于系统设置。您可以在 App 内编辑或删除个人计划，也可以通过系统设置关闭通知。

跟随天气时，App 请求 Open-Meteo 的固定东莞市天气，不读取设备定位，也不发送身份、成绩或课表。天气服务可能处理普通连接信息，包括 IP 地址和请求参数；您可以在 App 环境设置切换为手动预览。当前 App 不使用广告或跨 App 跟踪。

信息保留与您的选择

退出校宝前，可先移除已保存的登录；App 内退出或清除本机校宝登录资料用于清除本机校园访问状态。清理 TSchedule 不会删除学校持有的原始记录；查阅或更正校务记录请走学校现有渠道。有关校宝网页本身怎样处理资料，请咨询其服务提供方。

如果您来信，我们使用邮件地址与邮件内容回应问题，并按处理来信及适用法律所需保留。请不要发送密码、验证码、Cookie、成绩或能识别学生的截图。用户、家长或监护人可以使用上面的邮箱提出隐私问题，或就支持邮件和适用的数据权利与我们联系。

如果数据处理方式改变，我们会更新本页及生效日期。

TSchedule Privacy Policy

TSchedule is an independent iPad school companion. This policy covers the app and its support email. TSchedule has no separate user registration and is not an official app of a school or its portal provider. An existing school account is required to view academic records.

School sign-in and records

If you connect to the school, you sign in on its official portal page opened inside the app. The portal processes authentication, sessions, and requests directly. The app uses that session to display the current student’s authorized timetable, assignments, released grades, attendance, and other available records on the iPad. It does not edit school records or relay real portal credentials or academic records to a developer-operated server. Native views use real academic records within the current session rather than creating a separate long-term grade archive. The portal and WebKit may retain browser website data on the device depending on sign-in settings.

Optional saved sign-in

Saved sign-in is off by default. If you enable it under Settings → Personal Info → Campus Data, the confirmed school account credential is stored in the iPad’s device-only Keychain and does not synchronize across devices. If you allow sign-in on opening the app, or deliberately visit or refresh GPA, the app first checks the existing session. If needed, it can use the credential once on the official login page. Older enrollments do not gain startup permission automatically. It is not used for background polling or sent to the publisher. You can choose Remove Saved Sign-In in Settings or sign out of the school portal to clear local sign-in state.

Local plans and weather

The optional local nickname, personal plans, appearance and reminder preferences are stored on the device. The nickname does not edit your school profile. If you start a timetable Live Activity, the next course name, minutes remaining, and custom heading can appear on the Lock Screen. Notification visibility depends on your system settings. You can edit or delete your own plans in the app and turn off notifications in system settings.

When city weather is enabled, the app requests a fixed Dongguan weather report from Open-Meteo. It does not request device location or send school identity or records to that provider. The provider may process ordinary connection metadata, including IP addresses and request parameters. You can switch to manual weather previews in the appearance controls. The current app does not use advertising or cross-app tracking.

Retention and choices

You can remove the saved sign-in before signing out. Sign out or clear local school sign-in data in the app to remove the local school access state. Clearing TSchedule does not delete original school records; use your school’s usual channels to access or correct those. Ask your portal provider about its handling of portal data.

If you email support, we use your email address and message to respond and retain them as needed to handle the request and comply with applicable law. Do not send passwords, verification codes, cookies, grades, or screenshots that identify a student. Students, parents, and guardians can contact the publisher about privacy, support correspondence, or applicable data rights.

If these practices change, we will update this page and the effective date.
