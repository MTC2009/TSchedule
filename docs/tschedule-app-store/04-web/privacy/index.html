TSchedule 隐私政策

讨论稿，尚未生效。正式发布者和生效日期将在发布前确认。
最近修订：2026-11-21

TSchedule（澜图）是独立的学生 iPad 工具，不是学校或校宝的官方应用，不另设学生账号。下面区分了校宝、你的 iPad 和项目维护者各自能接触的信息。

校宝登录与学习资料

普通登录在 App 内的校宝官方网页完成。校宝收到登录请求并决定该账号可以查看什么。TSchedule 使用同一网页会话，在 iPad 上核对当前学生身份，将该账号有权查看的资料整理成原生页面。登录凭据、网页会话和学习资料不经过开发者服务器；原生读取逻辑不提取网页密码或导出 Cookie。

读取哪些内容、什么时候读

首次确认本人账号后，App 在前台读取少量相邻教学周，与本学年的高中校历核对，保存能确认的轮转课程及按日期发生的事项。「今日」和「计划」读同一份本机课表；只有在设置点「更新课表」才重新获取。任务、考勤和其他目录仍在前台按需更新，不下载附件。显示已缓存的「今日」时，有效的本人会话可更新本学期任务、考勤和已公布的 GPA 总览；近期成绩可复用，任务变化会使其失效。这个更新不代登录。历史学期和单科明细仍按选择读取。原生功能不提交作业、不修改考勤或成绩。

本机代登录是可选项

默认关闭，不开启也能在校宝网页登录。若在「设置 → 个人信息 → 校园资料」主动开启，App 从已核验的本人账号取得登录名，接收你在本机输入的密码，保存在设有锁屏密码的这台 iPad 的系统钥匙串中，不同步到 iCloud。先用已有会话；只有明确需要重新登录，才在获准的前台启动恢复或主动查看 GPA 时向官方登录页提交一次。拒绝或无法确认结果时不反复尝试。请不要在共用设备上开启。

保留期限、退出与删除

课表按账号和学年保存在这台 iPad 受系统文件保护、不参与设备备份的文件中；拥有设备解锁权限的人可能看到它。重开、断网或学校会话过期后仍可查看。任务、成绩和考勤只保存在当前会话内存，不建立长期成绩档案。打开完整校宝会暂时隐藏课表，核对为同一账号后恢复；确认换号或在 App 内退出／清除校宝登录资料，会撤销并清理旧课表。退出／清除也清理网站数据及绑定，不删除学校原始记录。单独移除已保存的登录只撤销代登录，不清除课表或退出网页。

个人计划、提醒与锁屏

个人计划、昵称与偏好保存在设备上；这些内容是否随设备备份，取决于系统和你的备份设置。退出校宝不会删除计划；请在对应账号的「计划」中删除，昵称可在设置里清空。主动开启实时活动或个人计划提醒后，下一节课程名、剩余分钟、自定义标题或提醒内容可能出现在锁屏，旁人可能看见。可选的课前提醒只用本机已确认的课表，不额外连接校宝。它只显示上课时间提示，不含课程名、教室或账号。退出或换号会清理旧提醒。可结束实时活动、关闭提醒，或在 iPad 系统设置中调整显示。

天气与开发者能看到的信息

跟随城市天气时，iPad 向 Open-Meteo 请求东莞固定坐标的天气，不读取设备定位，也不向它发送账号、课表或成绩。该服务能收到 IP 地址和请求参数等连接信息，按其自身政策处理。可以关闭「跟随东莞天气」，改用手动预览。本版没有广告或跨 App 跟踪。只有你主动发支持邮件时，维护者的 Gmail 邮箱才会收到你的发件地址和邮件内容。来信仅为处理问题及履行适用义务所需而保留，不用来建立学生档案。

未成年用户和联系渠道

如试用涉及不满十四周岁的学生，在学校和发布者落实适用的监护人同意及专门处理规则前，请不要连接真实校宝账号。App 当前没有监护人同意流程。学生或监护人可通过文末邮箱询问、要求更正或删除发给维护者的信息；学校记录请向学校查阅或更正。反馈只需版本、现象和操作步骤，请勿发送密码、验证码、Cookie 或能识别学生的截图。若处理用途或接收方发生实质变化，应先更新说明，并在适用时重新取得同意。

TSchedule Privacy Policy

Review draft, not yet in effect. The publisher and effective date will be confirmed before release.
Last revised: 2026-11-21

TSchedule is an independent student app for iPad, not an official app of the school or its portal provider. It does not create a separate student account. This policy distinguishes what the school portal, your iPad and the project maintainer can see.

School sign-in and academic records

For ordinary sign-in you use the official school portal inside the app. The portal receives the request and controls access. TSchedule uses that same web session on the iPad to verify the current student and display records available to that account. Credentials, web sessions and academic records are not relayed through a developer server. The native reader does not extract passwords from the page or export cookies.

What the app reads and when

After verifying the student, the app compares a small number of nearby teaching weeks with the high-school calendar and saves corroborated rotating lessons and dated entries. Today and Planner read the same device copy. Only Update Timetable in Settings retrieves a replacement. Tasks, attendance and other directories remain foreground reads; attachments are not downloaded. When cached Today is displayed, a verified session may refresh current-term tasks, attendance and the published GPA overview. Recent grades may be reused unless the task snapshot changes. This update cannot sign in for you. Historical terms and course details are loaded only when selected. Native reads do not submit work or edit attendance or marks.

Saved sign-in is optional

This option is off by default; ordinary portal sign-in works without it. If you turn it on in Settings → Personal Info → Campus Data, the app obtains the login name of the verified account and accepts a password you enter on the device. The pair is kept in this passcode-protected iPad’s system Keychain and does not sync to iCloud. The app first tries the existing session. Only when sign-in is required may an authorized foreground startup or a deliberate GPA visit submit the pair once on the official page. A refusal or uncertain result does not trigger repeated attempts. Do not enable this option on a shared device.

Retention, sign-out and deletion

The timetable is stored by account and academic year in a file on this iPad with system file protection and excluded from device backups. Someone who can unlock the iPad may see it after relaunch, offline or after the school session expires. Tasks, grades and attendance remain in session memory; the app does not build a lasting grade archive. Opening the full portal hides the timetable until the same account is confirmed. An account change, signing out in the app or clearing local school sign-in data revokes and clears the old timetable. Signing out or clearing also removes portal website data and the binding, not the school’s records. Removing saved sign-in alone revokes only the credential, not the timetable or web session.

Personal plans, notifications and the Lock Screen

Personal plans, a nickname and preferences are stored on the device. Device backups depend on the system and your backup settings. Signing out does not delete plans; delete them in Planner under the relevant account. You can clear the nickname in Settings. If you start a Live Activity or schedule a personal reminder, the next class name, remaining minutes, custom heading or reminder text may be visible to others on the Lock Screen. Optional class alerts use only the confirmed timetable on this iPad, without contacting the school. The alert contains timing only, not a class name, room or account. Signing out or changing accounts clears the old alerts. You can end the activity, turn off reminders or adjust visibility in iPad settings.

Weather and what the developer can see

When city weather is enabled, the iPad requests weather for fixed Dongguan coordinates from Open-Meteo. It does not read the device’s location or send account details, timetables or grades to this provider. Open-Meteo can receive the IP address and request parameters and handles them under its own policy. You can turn off city weather and use a manual preview. This version has no advertising or cross-app tracking. Only if you email support does the maintainer’s Gmail mailbox receive your sender address and message. Correspondence is retained only as needed to address the issue and applicable obligations, not to build a student profile.

Young students, questions and requests

If a pilot would include students under 14, do not connect a real school account before the school and publisher have put applicable guardian consent and specific data-handling rules in place. The app currently has no guardian-consent flow. Students and guardians can contact the maintainer below with privacy questions or requests to correct or delete information they sent. Contact the school about access to or correction of its records. For support, send only the version, symptom and steps—not passwords, verification codes, cookies or identifiable screenshots. Material changes to purposes or recipients should be explained before they take effect, with renewed consent where required.
