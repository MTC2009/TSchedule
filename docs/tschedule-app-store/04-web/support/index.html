常见问题

课表、作业或成绩没有更新
先确认网络正常，再检查校宝原网页是否已经显示该条记录。如果原网页正常而 TSchedule 没有显示，可以来信反馈。如果校宝原网页的记录有误，请通过学校原有渠道核实。
TSchedule 提示重新登录
如果校宝需要重新验证，请在 App 打开的校宝原网页完成登录。恢复登录后，回到刚才查看的页面。
如何关闭本机代登录？
如果开启过本机代登录，进入「设置 → 个人信息 → 校园资料」，点「移除已保存的登录」。如果也要结束当前的校宝会话，再点「退出校宝登录」。

Common questions

My timetable, assignments, or grades aren’t up to date.
Check your internet connection and look for the same record on the original school portal. If it appears there but not in TSchedule, contact us. If the portal itself shows the wrong information, use your school’s usual support channel.
TSchedule asks me to sign in again.
If the school portal requires you to sign in again, do so on the original portal page opened by the app. Then return to the page you were viewing.
How do I turn off saved sign-in?
If you enabled saved sign-in, go to Settings → 校园资料 (Campus Data) and choose 移除已保存的登录 (Remove Saved Sign-In). To end the current portal session as well, choose 退出校宝登录 (Sign Out of the School Portal).
