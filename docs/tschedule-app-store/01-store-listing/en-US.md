## Description

TSchedule is an independent iPad companion for a school day. See the next class, put your own plans beside the timetable, and check assignments, published grades, and attendance without hunting through different pages.

- **Today:** Browse an interactive timeline with class times and rooms when available.
- **Planner:** View the day or week and place personal plans alongside the schedule. Personal plans do not change school records.
- **My Tasks:** Find assignments, due dates, and published results. This app does not submit schoolwork.
- **GPA and attendance:** Review available results and course breakdowns. Estimates and practice calculations, where shown, are not official grades.

Connected academic data requires an existing student account on the supported Tsinglan School student portal. This is not an official app of the school or its portal provider. Screenshots contain no real student records.

The current app interface is in Simplified Chinese. Requires iPadOS 26 or later.

Confirm name availability in the publisher’s account. Use real contact URLs and the final signed build’s version, choose the age rating through the questionnaire, and remove internal notes. Do not describe login as permanently automatic or promise data when the school portal has not published it.
