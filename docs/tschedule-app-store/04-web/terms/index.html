这些条款说明学生怎样使用 TSchedule，以及它不能替代什么。校宝和学校仍负责原始校务记录及账号权限；安装本 App 不代表学校或校宝为它背书，也不授予任何新的访问权。

谁可以连接校宝

只在学校及校宝适用规则允许的范围内，用你本人有权使用的学生账号连接。不得共享账号、代收同学密码、查看他人资料或规避访问限制。若你发现了不属于自己的资料，请停止查看，不要转发，向学校及维护者报告现象即可。未成年用户可与监护人一同阅读；对于不满十四周岁的学生，适用隐私政策所述的试用限制。

学校记录与个人工具

TSchedule 是辅助查看和个人计划工具。课表变更、作业提交状态、考勤、更正和最终成绩以学校正式通知或校宝原记录为准。个人计划、提醒和 GPA 试算不会改写校宝，也不构成正式提交。遇到截止时间、考试或数据冲突，请向学校核对，不要只依赖通知。

你的选择与使用边界

本机代登录不是使用条件，只应在你能控制的个人 iPad 上开启。可随时移除绑定、退出校宝或停用 App。不要把考试材料、同学资料或带身份的截图公开传播。仓库的开源许可只涉及它覆盖的软件，不会赋予学校内容、标识或服务接口的使用许可。

服务变化与问题处理

网络、校宝页面或账号权限变化可能延迟或中断显示。若发生串号、异常请求或学校要求暂停，不应继续用受影响的连接试用；可先回到学校原有渠道，联系维护者核查。维护者可暂停提供受影响的版本或功能，这并不意味着能远程删除已安装副本。本条款不排除适用法律赋予你的权利或依法不得免除的责任。

条款、隐私与联系

隐私政策说明不同数据的用途、接收方、保留及移除方式。这份服务说明不能替代学校或校宝的规定，也不能代替依法需要的同意。若未来经 App Store 分发，适用的软件许可另行依该分发渠道说明。重要变化会标明修订及生效日期。问题请通过文末邮箱提出，无需提供学校密码或成绩。

These terms describe how to use TSchedule and what it cannot replace. The school and its portal remain responsible for the original records and account permissions. Installation does not imply their endorsement or grant new access rights.

Who may connect

Connect only with your own authorized student account and within the applicable school and portal rules. Do not share accounts, collect classmates’ passwords, access someone else’s records or evade access controls. If you see records that are not yours, stop viewing them, do not forward them and report the issue without including the records. Minors can review these terms with a guardian. The under-14 pilot restriction in the Privacy Policy applies.

Official records and personal tools

TSchedule assists with viewing records and planning. Use official school notices or the original portal for schedule changes, submission status, attendance, corrections and final grades. A personal plan, reminder or GPA estimate does not update school records or count as a submission. Confirm deadlines, exams and discrepancies with the school rather than relying only on a notification.

Your choices and limits

Saved sign-in is not required and should be enabled only on a personal iPad you control. You can remove the binding, sign out or stop using the app. Do not publicly distribute exam material, classmates’ information or identifiable screenshots. The repository’s open-source license covers the software within its scope, not the school’s content, marks or service access.

Changes, interruptions and support

Network outages, portal changes or changed permissions can delay or interrupt access. If records appear under the wrong account, requests behave abnormally or the school asks for a pause, stop using the affected connection, use the school’s usual channels and contact the maintainer. The maintainer may suspend distribution of an affected version or feature; this does not mean installed copies can be remotely deleted. These terms do not exclude rights or liability that cannot be waived under applicable law.

Terms, privacy and contact

The Privacy Policy explains uses, recipients, retention and removal. These service terms do not replace the school’s or portal’s rules or legally required consent. If the app is distributed through the App Store, the applicable software license will be identified through that channel. Material revisions will identify their revision and effective dates.
